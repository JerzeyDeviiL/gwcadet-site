Support

How to reach us and answers to common questions.

Contact us

Email admin@gwcadet.com for help, billing questions, bug reports or feature requests. We usually reply within one business day. Please include your Google Workspace™ domain, what you clicked, and the exact message GW Cadet showed.

Getting started

  1. Install GW Cadet from the Google Workspace™ Marketplace.
  2. Open any Google Sheets™ spreadsheet and choose Extensions > GW Cadet > Open sidebar.
  3. Pick an area, such as Users, and click List to write it into a sheet. Choose an action in a row's Action column, click Apply, and confirm.

Common questions

  • "Google refused this request because your account is not a Google Workspace administrator." GW Cadet works through the Google Admin console APIs, so it can only do what your account is allowed to do there. Sign in with a super admin account, or one with the admin role for the area you are managing (users, groups, org units, devices, resources or licenses).
  • The sidebar shows no Manage Account section. If several Google accounts are signed in to your browser, Google may run GW Cadet as your default account instead of the one in the spreadsheet. Use a browser window signed in only to your Workspace admin account.
  • The free trial. Every Workspace domain gets 50 free changes, with no card needed. Listing data is always free, and only changes that succeed count.
  • Subscriptions, seats and invoices. Open Manage Account in the sidebar. The person who bought the subscription can change the number of admin seats, update the card, see invoices or cancel there. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Schools and nonprofits. Email us from your school or nonprofit domain to unlock the discounted yearly price.
