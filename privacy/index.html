Privacy Policy

How GW Cadet and gwcadet.com handle information.

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WolfLoop Technology ("WolfLoop", "we", "us") handles information in connection with GW Cadet, a Google Sheets add-on for Google Workspace administrators (the "Add-on"), its licensing and billing service, and its website, gwcadet.com.

Summary

  • The Add-on works inside Google: it reads and changes your Google Workspace directory through Google's APIs and shows the results in your own Google Sheet. Your directory data (users, groups, devices, passwords and so on) is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • To run free trials, subscriptions and admin seats, the Add-on talks to our licensing service. It sends only what that needs: your organization's domain, the signed-in administrator's email address, and how many changes were made.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your full card details.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

Who uses the Add-on

The Add-on is installed and run by administrators of a Google Workspace organization (for example, a school or business) to manage that organization's accounts. The organization decides which data is managed and why, and it remains responsible for that data. For data about the people in its directory, the organization is the controller. The Add-on is a tool the organization's administrator operates.

What the Add-on accesses in Google

When an administrator runs the Add-on, it uses the permissions the administrator grants on Google's consent screen to access the following through Google's Admin SDK, Licensing, Groups Settings and Sheets APIs:

  • Users: names, email addresses, organizational unit, status (for example, suspended or admin), and other directory profile fields the administrator chooses to view or change. When the administrator resets a password, the new password is set too.
  • Custom user fields: the organization's own directory fields (for example, an Employee ID) and their values.
  • Groups: group names, descriptions, members and their roles, and group settings (for example, who can post or join).
  • Organizational units: the organization's unit structure.
  • ChromeOS devices: device records such as serial number, model, status, assigned user and organizational unit.
  • Calendar resources: meeting rooms and equipment, with their buildings and floors.
  • Licenses: which Google product licenses each account holds.
  • Audit reports (read-only): recent activity records the administrator chooses to view, for example sign-in events. These can include the time, the account, the event type and the IP address of the activity.
  • The open spreadsheet only: the single Google Sheet the Add-on is open in, where it writes results and reads the changes the administrator types. It cannot open any other file in Google Drive.
  • Who is signed in: the administrator's Google account email address and a Google-signed sign-in token (the `openid` and `userinfo.email` permissions). These are used only to tell our licensing service which organization and administrator is using the Add-on.

The Add-on does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive files or Calendar events.

How the data is used

Directory data is used only to:

  1. show it to the administrator in their own Google Sheet; and
  2. make the changes the administrator explicitly asks for, such as suspending a user, creating a group or assigning a license. Each change is confirmed by the administrator before it runs.

The Add-on does nothing on its own. It acts only when an administrator runs a command.

Our licensing service

The Add-on checks with our licensing service, at license.gwcadet.com, whether your organization has free-trial changes left or an active subscription, and whether the signed-in administrator has one of your organization's admin seats.

What the Add-on sends to it:

  • a sign-in token issued and signed by Google, from which we read your organization's domain and the administrator's email address; and
  • after a batch of changes, the number of changes that succeeded (for example, "3"). Not what they were, and not who they affected.

What the licensing service stores:

  • your organization's domain, its plan, how many free-trial changes it has used, and the date its paid access runs until;
  • the email address of each administrator holding an admin seat, with the dates they first and last used the Add-on;
  • the email address of the billing contact (the administrator who bought the subscription);
  • your Stripe customer ID, and whether your organization is approved for school or nonprofit pricing.

It never receives your directory data, spreadsheet contents, passwords or Google access tokens.

Why: to count free-trial changes per organization, to give access to paying organizations and their seated administrators, to let the billing contact see and free seats, and to connect your organization to its Stripe subscription.

Where: the licensing service and its database are hosted by Render (render.com) in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

How long: seat records are deleted when the billing contact frees the seat. Domain records are kept while your organization uses GW Cadet. After a subscription ends we keep the domain record for 24 months so a lapsed organization cannot restart the free trial, and so we can answer billing questions, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see "Your choices and rights").

Payments

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe, which acts as the merchant of record (reseller) for GW Cadet purchases. When you check out, you give your payment details, billing name, address and email directly to Stripe on Stripe's own pages, under the Stripe Privacy Policy. Stripe also calculates and collects sales tax, VAT or GST where it applies. We never receive or store your full card number. We receive from Stripe your customer ID, subscription status, plan, number of seats, billing period and the billing email, and use them only to manage your subscription.

Where data is stored

  • In your spreadsheet: everything the Add-on lists, and the result of every change, is written into the administrator's own Google Sheet. That can include temporary passwords the administrator generated. The sheet belongs to your organization and is stored by Google under your organization's Google Workspace agreement. You control who can see it, how long it is kept and when it is deleted.
  • In our licensing service: only the licensing and billing records listed above.
  • At Stripe: payment and billing details, as described under "Payments".
  • Error diagnostics: if the Add-on hits an unexpected error, Google Apps Script records the error message in our Google Cloud project's logs so we can fix the problem. An error message can occasionally include an identifier, such as the email address involved in the failed operation. The licensing service keeps similar technical logs (time, domain and error) for the same purpose. These logs are kept for up to 30 days, and only WolfLoop's developers can read them. They are used for nothing else.

We do not keep any other copy of your data.

This website and the waitlist

  • The website: gwcadet.com is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. As the host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The waitlist: if you join the waitlist, we collect only the email address you enter. It is kept in WolfLoop's own Google Workspace account, either in a Google Form and Sheet or in our admin@gwcadet.com mailbox. We use it only to tell you when GW Cadet is ready to install and to send major launch news, and we never sell or share it. To leave the list or have your address deleted, email admin@gwcadet.com.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the service providers that run GW Cadet, and only what each needs:

  • Stripe, to sell and bill subscriptions (as described under "Payments");
  • Render, which hosts our licensing service and its database; and
  • Google, whose services the Add-on runs on.

We do not transfer your Google user data to any other third party. We do not use it for advertising, for creditworthiness or lending decisions, or to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. No person at WolfLoop reads your data, except in these cases:

  • when you ask us for support and give us permission;
  • when we review a request for school or nonprofit pricing that you send us; or
  • when needed for security purposes or to comply with the law.

Google API Services: Limited Use

GW Cadet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we protect your data

Some of the data the Add-on handles is sensitive: account details, temporary passwords the administrator generates, IP addresses in audit reports, and information about students. We protect it with these measures:

  • Directory data stays in Google: every request between the Add-on and Google's APIs travels over HTTPS (TLS) inside Google's infrastructure. The only outside service the Add-on contacts is our licensing service, and it sends that service only the sign-in token and change counts described above.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest: connections to the licensing service and Stripe use HTTPS. Your Google Sheet, our error logs and the licensing database are encrypted at rest by their providers.
  • No directory copies held by us: we never receive, store or see your directory data, your passwords or your Google access tokens. Google issues and protects the access token the Add-on uses, and it expires automatically.
  • Verified identity: the licensing service accepts only sign-in tokens signed by Google for GW Cadet. Your domain is taken from that token, never from anything the Add-on could alter, so no one can act on another organization's subscription.
  • Least privilege: the Add-on requests only the permissions its features need. It uses the read-only permission for audit reports, and it can open only the one spreadsheet it is running in, not your other Drive files.
  • Administrator-only access: Google's Admin SDK enforces administrator privileges on every request. The Add-on can only do what the signed-in administrator is already allowed to do in the Google Admin console, and a non-administrator gets nothing.
  • Human confirmation: every change is listed in a confirmation dialog, and nothing runs until the administrator approves it. Irreversible actions, such as deleting an account, carry an extra warning.
  • Passwords: a generated password is shown only in the Result column of the administrator's own sheet. It is never logged or sent anywhere. We advise administrators to copy it to the user and then clear the cell.
  • Restricted internal access: only WolfLoop's developers can read the licensing database and the logs, and only to run and fix the service.
  • Fails safe: if the licensing service cannot be reached, the Add-on keeps working rather than block you mid-task.
  • Incident response: if we learn of a security incident affecting data handled by GW Cadet, we will notify affected customers without undue delay and take steps to contain and fix it.

Students and children

The Add-on is used by administrators, not by children. Some organizations, such as schools, may manage student accounts with it. In that case the school controls that information and decides how it is used, under the laws that apply to it. We do not collect any information from or about students; the licensing service stores only administrators' email addresses.

Your choices and rights

  • Revoke access: remove the Add-on's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. A Google Workspace administrator can also uninstall the Add-on or block it for the whole organization.
  • Free a seat: the billing contact can remove an administrator's seat from the Add-on's Manage Account panel, which deletes that seat record.
  • Manage billing: update payment details, download invoices or cancel in the Stripe billing page linked from the Manage Account panel.
  • Delete data: delete the spreadsheet, or the rows you no longer need. To have your organization's licensing records deleted, email us. If you still have an active subscription, we will cancel it first.
  • Access and correction: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to how it is used. We hold none of your directory data, so most requests about it should go to your organization's administrator. For the licensing and billing records described above, or any other privacy question, contact us at the address below. Requests about payment details go to Stripe.

International use

The Add-on can be used anywhere Google Workspace is available. Directory data is processed by Google on its infrastructure, under your organization's agreement with Google. Licensing records are processed by Render in the United States, and payment data by Stripe, which may process data in several countries. WolfLoop is based in Canada.

Changes to this policy

If we change how GW Cadet handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If the change is material, we will give notice in the Add-on or on our website.
